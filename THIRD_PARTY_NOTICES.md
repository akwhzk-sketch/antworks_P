## Fitted winter garment (cycle01)

The fitted coat shell derives its selected body/arm triangles and skin weights from the existing CC0 Quaternius human assets documented above and in docs/FREE_RESOURCES_0.8.json. The woven canvas texture, padded offset, pocket/zip detail and wetness visualization are generated by original project code in src/winter-garment.ts. No additional external asset was downloaded. Existing source, license and transformation records remain applicable. This is a fitted skinned garment, not a cloth physics simulation.
